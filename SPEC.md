# ReelHook — Spec ## 1. Concept & Vision ReelHook is the hook and title optimizer for TikTok and YouTube Shorts creators. The first 2 seconds of a short-form video decide everything — most creators stare at a blank title field and guess. ReelHook AI generates 10 scroll-stopping hook options and 5 title variants ranked by predicted hook score, so creators ship faster and win more often. **Tagline:** First 2 seconds. Win or lose. ## 2. Design Language ### Aesthetic Direction High-energy, punchy dark UI — cyberpunk meets creator-economy. Think terminal-grade dark with neon accents that feel native to TikTok/Shorts energy. ### Color Palette - Background: `#0a0a0a` - Surface: `#141414` - Border: `#262626` - Primary (cyan): `#00f2ea` — TikTok energy - Secondary (pink): `#ff0080` — YouTube Shorts energy - Success: `#00c851` - Text: `#ffffff` - Muted: `#666666` ### Typography - Font: **Inter** (Google Fonts) - Headings: Bold, large, punchy - Body: Regular weight, clean ### Motion Philosophy - Entrance animations: fade + slide-up, staggered 50ms - Hover states: scale + glow on cards - Demo loading: skeleton shimmer effect - Micro-interactions: button press feedback, score bar animations ### Visual Assets - Icons: Lucide React (consistent stroke-width: 2) - Decorative: gradient accents, glowing borders on primary CTAs ## 3. Layout & Structure **Page Flow:** 1. **NavBar** — sticky, logo left, links center, CTA right 2. **Hero** — full viewport height, centered, headline + sub + demo input + CTA button 3. **Hook Demo** — interactive mock showing the product experience 4. **Features Grid** — 2x3 grid of feature cards 5. **How It Works** — 3-step horizontal flow 6. **Stats Bar** — 4 numbers showing social proof 7. **Testimonials** — 3 cards with creator names and follower growth 8. **Pricing** — 3-column cards (Free / Starter / Growth) 9. **CTA** — full-width banner 10. **Footer** — minimal links **Responsive:** Mobile-first, single-column on small screens. ## 4. Features & Interactions ### NavBar - Logo: "ReelHook" with hook icon - Links: Hooks, Pricing (scroll to sections) - Login button (ghost style) - "Try Free" primary CTA button ### Hero Section - Headline: "First 2 seconds.
